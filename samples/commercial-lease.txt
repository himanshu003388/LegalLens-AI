COMMERCIAL TRIPLE NET (NNN) LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into as of November 15, 2025, by and between Metro Commercial Properties LLC ("Landlord"), and Urban Bloom Bakery LLC ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES & LEASE TERM
Landlord leases to Tenant Suite 204 located at 742 Evergreen Plaza, comprising approximately 2,400 rentable square feet. The initial lease term is five (5) years, commencing on January 1, 2026.

2. BASE RENT & AUTOMATIC ANNUAL ESCALATION
Tenant shall pay Base Rent in the amount of $6,500.00 per month, due in advance on the first (1st) day of each calendar month. On each anniversary of the Commencement Date, the Base Rent shall automatically escalate by seven percent (7.0%) over the preceding year's rent.

3. TRIPLE NET (NNN) EXPENSES & COMMON AREA MAINTENANCE (CAM)
This is an absolute Triple Net Lease. In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share (18.5%) of all Real Estate Taxes, Building Property Insurance, and Common Area Maintenance (CAM) fees. Tenant accepts these charges without audit rights and agrees to pay any reconciliation deficit within ten (10) calendar days of invoicing.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT & LETTER OF CREDIT
Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $26,000.00 (equivalent to 4 months rent) as security for full performance. In the event of any default, Landlord may retain 100% of the deposit as liquidated damages without forfeiting any other legal remedies.

5. MAINTENANCE, ROOF & STRUCTURAL REPAIRS
Tenant shall, at its sole cost and expense, keep and maintain in good order, condition, and repair the entire Premises, including all HVAC systems, electrical, plumbing, grease traps, plate glass windows, and non-structural fixtures. Tenant shall further bear proportionate cost for any major roof replacement or parking lot resurfacing executed by Landlord during the term.

6. ASSIGNMENT, SUBLETTING & CHANGE OF CONTROL
Tenant shall not assign, mortgage, pledge, or sublet the Premises or any part thereof without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold in its sole and absolute discretion. Any change in majority ownership or voting control of Tenant shall constitute a prohibited assignment.

7. INDEMNITY, EXCULPATION & CASUALTY
Tenant agrees to indemnify, defend, and save harmless Landlord from any liability, injury, or damage occurring on the Premises. Landlord shall have zero liability to Tenant for any theft, interruption of business, water damage, power outages, or HVAC failure, regardless of fault or negligence.

8. PERSONAL GUARANTY
The performance of all Tenant obligations under this Lease is unconditionally and irrevocably guaranteed by Jane Doe, an individual residing at 42 Oak Ridge Way, who personally assumes joint and several liability for all rental payments, repairs, and default damages throughout the entire 5-year term.
