RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on June 1, 2024, between Oakwood Property Management LLC ("Landlord"), and Alex Johnson ("Tenant"), for the leased premises located at Apt 4B, 742 Evergreen Terrace, Springfield, IL ("Premises").

1. TERM OF LEASE
The initial term shall commence on July 1, 2024 ("Commencement Date"), and continue for twelve (12) consecutive months, ending on June 30, 2025. 
AUTOMATIC RENEWAL: Unless Tenant provides at least sixty (60) days' prior written notice of intent to vacate prior to the expiration date, this Lease shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms at a rent increase of 8% per annum.

2. RENT AND PAYMENT TERMS
Tenant agrees to pay monthly rent in the amount of $2,200.00 USD, due on the first (1st) day of each calendar month. Payments received after the fifth (5th) day of the month shall incur a mandatory late fee of $150.00 plus 5% monthly compounding interest on the overdue balance.

3. SECURITY DEPOSIT
Upon signing, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of $4,400.00 (equivalent to two months' rent) as a security deposit. Landlord reserves the right to retain the entire security deposit if Tenant terminates the Lease prior to the expiration of the full term, irrespective of whether the unit is subsequently re-rented.

4. MAINTENANCE, REPAIRS, AND INSPECTIONS
Tenant shall keep the Premises in a clean and sanitary condition. Landlord shall have the right to enter the Premises at any time without prior notice for routine inspections, maintenance, or showing the unit to prospective buyers or future tenants.

5. INDEMNIFICATION AND LIABILITY ALLOCATION
Tenant agrees to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Landlord, its agents, and employees from and against any and all claims, liabilities, personal injury damages, property loss, and legal defense costs arising from Tenant's use or occupancy of the Premises, even if such injury or damage is caused in whole or in part by the Landlord's ordinary negligence or building defect.

6. ALTERATIONS AND PETS
Tenant shall not perform any painting, structural alterations, or keep any pets on the Premises without express written consent from Landlord. Violation of this provision shall incur a penalty of $500.00 per occurrence.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
In the event Tenant vacates or terminates prior to the conclusion of the 12-month term, Tenant remains liable for all remaining monthly rent payments through the end of the Lease term as accelerated liquidated damages, in addition to forfeiture of the full security deposit.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION
This Lease shall be governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. Any disputes or claims arising out of this Lease shall be resolved exclusively through binding arbitration administered in Chicago, Illinois. Tenant hereby knowingly waives any right to a trial by jury.
