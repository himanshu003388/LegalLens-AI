MASTER SAAS SERVICES AGREEMENT (VERSION 2.0 - REVISED)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is dated January 15, 2024, between CloudPulse Inc. ("Provider") and Vertex Enterprise Corp ("Customer").

1. SERVICES AND LICENSE
Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, revocable, non-transferable subscription right to access and use the CloudPulse Enterprise Analytics platform for internal business purposes during the Term.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
Customer shall pay all subscription fees specified in applicable Order Forms. Invoices are payable within fifteen (15) days of invoice receipt (Net 15). Late balances shall accrue interest at 2.5% per month compounded daily.

3. TERM, AUTO-RENEWAL, AND TERMINATION
The initial term of this Agreement shall be one (1) year. 
EVERGREEN AUTO-RENEWAL: This Agreement shall auto-renew automatically for successive 12-month periods unless Customer delivers written opt-out notice at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration date. Either party may terminate for cause with sixty (60) days' written notice.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
PROVIDER'S MAXIMUM AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL BE CAPPED AT THE FEES PAID IN THE PRECEDING THREE (3) MONTHS. CUSTOMER EXPRESSLY AGREES THAT CUSTOMER'S INDEMNIFICATION LIABILITIES SHALL REMAIN UNCAPPED AND UNLIMITED.

5. INDEMNIFICATION
Provider shall defend and indemnify Customer against third-party claims alleging that Customer's authorized use of the Services infringes any valid patent, copyright, or trademark of a third party, subject to Customer providing prompt written notice. Customer agrees to indemnify Provider for any operational misuse of data.

6. GOVERNING LAW AND ARBITRATION
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the State of Delaware. Any disputes shall be resolved through mandatory binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.
